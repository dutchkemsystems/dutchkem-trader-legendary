Core Intelligence

Six Engines of
Market Intelligence

Every signal is validated through six independent AI systems before it reaches your portfolio. No guesswork. No gambles. Just probability-weighted decisions backed by data.

🧠

Regime Detection

Our neural network classifies market conditions in real time — trending, mean-reverting, volatile, or calm. Strategies automatically adapt to the current regime, so you're never caught using a trend-following system in a ranging market.

💬

Sentiment Analysis

Natural language processing scans central bank statements, financial news, and social media sentiment across 12 languages. We quantify the crowd's bias so you can trade with the wind at your back — or fade it when conviction is extreme.

📊

ML Price Prediction

Gradient-boosted ensembles generate directional forecasts with calibrated confidence scores. Every prediction includes a time horizon and confidence interval, giving you not just "buy" but "how sure we are and for how long."

⚖️

Risk Parity Engine

Capital is allocated across 10 currency pairs using risk parity — not equal weights. Volatility-adjusted sizing ensures no single pair dominates your risk. Correlation filters prevent stacked exposures that blow up accounts.

📅

Economic Calendar

18 high-impact events tracked automatically. The engine enters blackout mode before ECB, FOMC, NFP, and CPI releases — flattening exposure and resuming only after volatility subsides. News events become edge, not danger.

🧬

Genetic Optimization

Strategies evolve through genetic algorithms that test thousands of parameter combinations against historical data. The fittest survive. The system self-optimizes weekly, adapting to changing market structure without human intervention.

The Process

From Signal to Execution
in Three Steps

No manual charting. No emotional decisions. Just a disciplined pipeline that turns market data into profitable trades.

02

Decide

The regime classifier determines market state. Risk parity weights optimal position sizes. The ML ensemble scores directional probability. Only high-conviction setups pass all filters.

03

Trades are placed with tier-based lot sizing, trailing stops at 1:1R breakeven, partial profit-taking at targets, and automatic drawdown protection. You manage strategy — the engine manages risk.
